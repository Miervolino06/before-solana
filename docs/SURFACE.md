# BEFORE surface

The surface treats a statement as an object worth keeping. Ice white paper, bright cobalt ink, and precise concentric contours evoke a record being pressed into permanence. Lavender is the studio surface around the object; green and pink are alternate paper stocks. Archivo Variable gives the big words weight, while Manrope carries the transactional details. This follows the selected geometric record-mastering sleeve direction from the product's design seed.

The live card is deliberately marked **DRAFT · UNSIGNED**. Its contours are deterministic from the text and category; after confirmation they also use the unique mint address. Exported SVG escapes user text and includes the mint address only for a confirmed record. The preview never presents a draft as chain proof.

The route is write → network quote → explicit review → wallet signature → chain confirmation → proof. Review displays the exact public memo, wallet, mint, one-unit supply, revoked mint authority, absence of freeze authority, live fee and rent estimate, total, and no app fee. Acknowledging public permanence is required before signing. Pending transactions keep their signature and provide a confirmation check so the user does not accidentally duplicate a record.

History is a list of transaction signatures on this device. Opening one fetches the public record from devnet. A share link stores only the signature in `?tx=` and fetches the record from chain. Confirmation sound is off by default and plays only on verified success. Motion reduces with system preference.

No counters, portfolio values, fabricated activity, or NFT metadata claims appear. Solana devnet and the faucet are named in the interface; a timestamp is described as evidence of publication, never of truth.
